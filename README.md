# WarDOGE

A free, meme-flavoured, top-down team shooter that runs in the browser. Play at [wardoge.ai](https://wardoge.ai).

Three teams fight over a Control Zone. Every 30 seconds the team with the most players inside scores a point, and the moving Hot Zone counts double. First to 20 wins.

## Features
- Huge random map with buildings, tanks, and buildable walls
- Loadout shop: rifle, shotgun, rocket launcher, sniper, helmet, armor, grenades, medkits
- Downed and revive system, healing, cash rewards for teamwork
- Limited vision: enemies only visible when close and in line of sight
- Guest play or local accounts with saved cash, level and match history
- Keyboard + mouse (layout independent) and touch controls on phones

## Run locally
Just open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
